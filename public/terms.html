Internlog does not pre-screen reviews before they appear on the Site. Reported reviews are reviewed by a real person — not an automated system — and we aim to review each report within 5 business days of submission.

If you are the subject of a review, or represent a company being reviewed, and believe a specific review is false, defamatory, violates these Terms, or should not be published, you can:
