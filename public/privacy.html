Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Internlog collects, why, and how it's used. We built the Site to require as little personal information as possible.

1. Information you provide

Reviews. When you submit a review, we collect: the company and role, your rating and difficulty-to-get score, optional hourly pay, optional location, optional internship start/end dates, and your written pros, cons, and day-in-the-life description. We do not ask for your name when you submit a review, and reviews are published without any attached identity.

Contact form and reports. If you use the Contact Us form or report a review, we collect the information you type in — which may include your name and always includes your email address, since we need it to respond to you.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not require account creation, so we do not collect passwords, usernames, or persistent profiles. We do not use cookies, browser local storage, tracking pixels, or third-party advertising or analytics trackers of any kind. If that changes in the future (for example, if we add basic analytics), we will update this policy first.

3. How we use information

  • Review content is published publicly on the Site, exactly as submitted (minus obvious violations we catch during moderation).
  • Every new review and every report is automatically sent to our internal email for manual moderation review — this is how we catch inappropriate or non-relevant content, since we don't pre-screen reviews before they go live.
  • Contact form and report submissions (including your email) are used only to respond to your request and are not shared with, or sold to, third parties.

4. Data retention

Reviews remain on the Site until removed through our moderation process. Contact form and report submissions are retained as long as needed to resolve the request, and may be kept afterward for our internal records of past moderation decisions.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. We do not share contact-form or report submissions with the companies being reviewed, except as necessary to resolve a specific dispute (for example, informing a company that a review was removed) — we would not share your email or identity with a company as part of that process without your consent.

6. Age requirement

Internlog is intended for users age 16 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you believe a user under 16 has submitted content, contact us and we will remove it.

7. Your choices

Since reviews are submitted without an account or name attached, we generally cannot identify which review belongs to you if you want it removed later — the fastest way to have a review taken down is to email us with as much identifying detail as possible (company, role, approximate date posted, and content) at internlogged@gmail.com.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information submitted to the Site, but no online service can guarantee perfect security. Please don't include sensitive personal information (like a Social Security number, financial account details, or another person's private information) in a review or message to us.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the Site evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: internlogged@gmail.com
